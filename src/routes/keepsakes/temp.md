I went into this show with incredibly low expectations, mainly bc the only thing I knew about it was that it was based on League of Legends, and my only exposure to LoL were the people in my year in high school who were obsessed with it and who I generally did not get along with or look up to. Suffice to say, the show knocked my socks off. It has some of the best writing, plot, and character development of any show I've seen. Of animated media, it is one of the most beautiful and artfully made, with some of the stills looking like they could be framed in a museum. It is progressive in a freeing and understated way that builds its own standalone world without defining it in relation/contrast to our own. I cried at several points in this show (s1e3, s1e9, s2e7, s2e8, s2e9) and felt a sense of grief when I finished it, which I've only feel for books and universes that felt so real I got lost in them.
