I have many projects and hobbies, and have loved each and every one of them. Looking back, I think they represent me well. Some of them include:

  • Knitting a lopapeysa, selbu mittens, and a cable knit hat
  • Making ceramics like teapots, fermentation crocks, and tumblers
  • Growing indigo dye and cigars from seed
  • Growing and arranging window boxes with my neighbor
  • Fermenting 1-year aged miso and cider
  • Playing piano (currently learning Ballade no. 1 in G minor, op. 23 by Chopin)
  • Performing improv with the Endgames Improv studio
  • Backpacking the JMT, TMB, ECT section (solo), and Collegiate Peaks West
  • Canoe tripping in Algonquin Park and the Boundary Waters
  • Trail running in Marin, the Santa Cruz Mountains, and NH, including completing a Presi Traverse in 8hrs 10min
  • Foraging for mushrooms with the Mycological Society of San Francisco
  • Organizing and writing in a fanfiction competition, designing and professionally printing the stories into 30pg booklets

If you have any shared interests, or think I might enjoy a project or hobby not featured here, please reach out.
